NoorNote

A Feature-Rich Nostr Desktop Client for Linux & macOS

NoorNote is a free, open-source Nostr client built for the desktop. Browse your timeline, write and discover long-form articles, manage follows, bookmarks and mute lists, send zaps, and handle your keys securely, all from a native app that respects your sovereignty.

NoorSigner

Desktop Key Signer for Nostr

Standalone App

NoorSigner is an independent desktop application that manages your Nostr private keys in a dedicated, secure process. It handles all signing operations so that your client never needs direct access to your secret key.

While it integrates seamlessly with NoorNote, fully controllable from within the client, NoorSigner is a standalone project that can be used with any Nostr desktop application that supports NIP-46 remote signing.

Mobile

NoorNote on your Smartphone

Works Today

NoorNote's web client runs inside Keychat's built-in browser on both Android and iOS. Keychat is a Nostr-based messenger with Signal-level encryption that includes a full web browser with extension support.

Open noornote.app in Keychat's browser, sign in with your Keychat identity, and you have a full Nostr client on your phone with your keys managed securely by Keychat itself.
