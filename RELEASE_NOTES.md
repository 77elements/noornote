# NoorNote 1.0.0

**A complete visual redesign: the new "Noor jadīd" look.**

This 1.0 milestone caps months of groundwork across the 0.x series: the move to native Electron desktop and Android builds, a modular architecture rebuild, and continuous performance, stability, and privacy improvements.

**New**
- New default theme "Noor jadīd": a deep purple-black palette with a single magenta accent.
- Profile content tabs (Articles, Videos, Products, Zapstore, Badges) that only appear when there's something to show. Articles as a grid, Videos as a media masonry, Zapstore apps as rich cards.
- New interaction bar now has a public bookmark toggle and post analytics.

**Changed**
- Redesigned note cards, the single-note thread view, profiles, and notifications throughout.
- Notifications now use color-coded type tiles, and zap amounts stand out in gold.
- New magenta app icons across web, desktop, and Android.

**Fixes**
- Profiles of people you don't follow now resolve reliably via the reposter's relays.
- The account switcher shows "Anonymous" instead of a raw key.
- Hardened signer timeouts and connectivity handling.
