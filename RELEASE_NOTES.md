# NoorNote 1.3.0

- **Live streams in your feed:** NIP-53 live activities now render as proper live cards across the timeline, notes, reposts, bookmarks and notifications.
- **Tag people in images (NIP-68):** add user tags to images with a chip composer; tagged users get notified.
- **Soft mute:** silence a user's notifications (replies, reactions, zaps on your notes) without hiding their posts, profile or replies anywhere else, synced via NIP-78.
- **Better messages & zaps:** a toast now alerts you to DMs from people you don't follow, and zaps carry a badge that opens their reply thread.
- **Relay insights + fixes:** the analytics modal shows which relays a note was seen on; Follow Pack reposts/quotes now render the full card, the extended-follows toggle no longer sticks off before login, and wallet storage is more resilient.
