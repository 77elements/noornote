# NoorNote 1.2.8

- **Nostrord group notifications fixed:** posts in your groups no longer get missed when a relay is slow. You can now optionally be alerted about your own posts too, and tapping a notification opens the group in the Nostrord web client.
- **Smoother side-column tabs:** the secondary column now has a clickable home tab, and stray tabs clean up correctly when you resize the window.
